Our Story

A practice built on community Redesigned

Silverdale Medical has been part of the Hibiscus Coast for over 30 years. What started as a small local practice has grown into one of the region's most trusted healthcare providers — but our values have stayed exactly the same.

We believe everyone deserves accessible, high-quality GP care close to home. That's why we've expanded to four locations across Silverdale, Millwater, and the surrounding area — so there's always a familiar face nearby, no matter where you are.

Our team of GPs, nurses, and support staff work together to give you joined-up care that goes beyond a single appointment. We know you by name, we know your history, and we're here for the long haul.
