Influenza Season 2026 — Vaccinations Available Improved visibility

Updated: 1 April 2026

Influenza activity is increasing across the Auckland region. The 2026 flu vaccine is now available at all four of our clinics. The vaccine is free for adults aged 65+, pregnant women, children aged 4 years and under who have been hospitalised for a respiratory illness, and people with eligible long-term conditions. Everyone else is welcome to receive the funded vaccine through their GP — just book an appointment and mention you'd like to be vaccinated.
